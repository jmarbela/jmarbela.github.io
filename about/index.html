About

Hi! My name is Julia Arbelaez.

Pretty much everyone calls me Jules, though. I'm a Division I college soccer player at Syracuse University, and a journalism student in the S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications.

As a student journalist and athlete, I've developed a passion for storytelling across different forms of media. I'm particularly interested in sports journalism and content creation, which is rooted in a desire to hear other people's stories while also finding ways to tell my own. I also have an interest in data journalism and using information and technology to tell important stories.

I aim to build a career in sports media, combining my background as a Division I athlete with my passion for communication. I'm excited to tell stories that offer news perspecives and connect with audiences. One day, I hope to be working as a Premier League reporter in London, England.
